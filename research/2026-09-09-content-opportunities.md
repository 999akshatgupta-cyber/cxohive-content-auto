## 2. The US room hired Americans the day before it froze India’s file.

**Final score:** 9.5 / 10  
**Evidence confidence:** HIGH on the news; LOW on qualified ICP LinkedIn comments  
**Source type:** News + Novelty  
**Scoring note:** Observed comments on the *PERM story itself* are a sales coach and a job-seeker. Parameters would cap this at 8.4. **Novelty option used.**

**Why the ICP cares**  
For a large slice of Indian IT SM–VP, the US room was the path to being “explainable” globally: H-1B, PERM, the person HQ can put on a plane. Cognizant’s PERM filings were suspended 8–9 Sep. The day before, the same company announced 1,500 US college hires and a 15,000-person US “Frontier” workforce. That is the global-room version of leftover: the next seat is being staffed in a different country.

**Guarantee (why this is a 9.5 for our use case)**  
An employed Director at Cognizant, TCS, Infosys, Wipro or HCL will feel this without a layoff headline. It is not a visa-sympathy post and not a recruiter pile-on. It names two files written in the same 24 hours: the US-graduate hiring file, and the frozen India green-card file. The next seat in India now has to be won in an India room. That is positioning + sponsorship, which is the product.

**Supporting evidence**

ET / IANS, 9 Sep 2026, 01:01 IST: same suspension. **PERM ≠ H-1B.** “Suspension of PERM filings does not, by itself, establish that a company’s H-1B petitions have also been suspended.” No charges named.
