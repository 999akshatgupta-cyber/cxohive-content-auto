## The email is a choice. The company says now is not the time.

1. **Content opportunity.** On 26 Oct 2026 Amy Coleman, Microsoft’s chief people officer, told employees that a Voluntary Exit Offer was open to eligible people at level 70 and below in participating teams in Canada, India, the United Kingdom, and the United States. The blog does not define level 70, does not give a headcount, and does not state a payout or a deadline. Times of India (Shilpa Phadnis, 27 Oct) and The Indian Express (Vidheesha Kuntamalla, updated 29 Oct) both put the India decision at 1 December. Express says Microsoft has said the India package is broadly similar to standard severance: one month of base salary for every completed year of service, health insurance, and career-transition support, and that the individual letter is what settles the terms. When TOI contacted Microsoft, the company said: “For most eligible employees, now won’t be the right time to consider what’s next outside of Microsoft, but we are giving them that option.” The only India employee on the record who says the whole team received the email is a 23-year-old Level 60 Azure engineer in Hyderabad, about three years in, who is staying. The post is about an employed India senior who is either inside that email or sitting next to a team that got it, in the week the company has already told most eligible people that leaving now is the wrong time, without a sentence on what staying is for.
