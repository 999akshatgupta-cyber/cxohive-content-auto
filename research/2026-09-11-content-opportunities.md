## 1. India runs a third of the drug pipeline. The Site Head still will not say India owns the strategy.

**Final score:** 8.4 / 10  
**Evidence confidence:** HIGH on the named facts; LOW on qualified ICP LinkedIn comments  
**Source type:** News (ETGCC) + operator interview

**Why the ICP cares**

**Supporting evidence**  
ETGCC, Debroop Roy, published 11 Sep 2026 16:45 IST, updated 18:37 IST. Interview with Ganpat Anchaliya, Site Head, Novartis Hyderabad.  
https://gcc.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/people-culture/at-25-novartis-corporate-center-in-hyderabad-bets-on-boomerangs-ai-and-global-ownership/134062673  

Facts the article directly establishes:  
- India operation: ~200 people when Anchaliya joined → nearly 9,000 now; 25 years in India; started 2001 with 20 people in data management, statistics, IT; moved Mumbai → Hyderabad 2007–08.  
- ~one-third of Novartis global drug-development operations managed from Hyderabad (Anchaliya).  
- Wet lab at Genome Valley: ~350 people; India ~40% of Novartis technical research capabilities (Anchaliya).  
- ~60% of Novartis IT talent and scope of work “managed and delivered through India teams”; finance 600–700 in Hyderabad.  
- Biomedical research group in Hyderabad ~200 people (last 2–3 years).  
- Some Hyderabad-based leaders now run global functions; global head of IT infrastructure is based there.  
- 15% of employees are boomerangs — people who left and returned. “Some leave for better titles or opportunities elsewhere.”  
- Anchaliya: eight roles in 17 years. “No other company will give me eight different roles in 17 years.”  
- Single-digit attrition (Anchaliya); 800–900 hires/year; 8,000+ applications per job.  
- AI: India ~one-third of company bronze AI certifications and nearly 40% of silver (Anchaliya). Hiring prompt engineers and data scientists; “redesigning roles that can be automated.”  
- Page shows “Read Comment (1)”. Comment text unavailable.

**Direct quotes (Anchaliya, ETGCC):**  
- “When we started, it was more of an operational centre. Today, it's a very key element of the entire work chain for Novartis globally.”  
- “If you have a significant part of that particular process sitting at one location, the teams can start to also transform the processes.”  
- Reporter: “Anchaliya is careful not to claim that India owns Novartis' strategy. That still sits with the global headquarters and senior leadership.”  
- “We have evolved to that phase where some of our leaders through their capabilities and talent manage work across the borders.”  
- “The complete scope of Novartis globally is sitting here.”
