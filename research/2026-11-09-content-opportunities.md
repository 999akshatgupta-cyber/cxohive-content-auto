Full post text:

90% of developers now use AI at work.
Most engineering orgs still aren’t shipping meaningfully faster.
That’s not a tooling problem. It’s an SDLC problem.
Here’s what the data says:
→ DORA 2025 (~5,000 respondents): AI adoption now correlates with higher throughput, but also higher delivery instability. Their conclusion is that AI is an amplifier. It magnifies strong systems and exposes weak ones.
→ Faros AI (10,000+ developers, 1,255 teams): high-adoption teams merged 98% more PRs. Review time rose 91%, PR size rose 154%, and org-level delivery metrics stayed flat.
→ METR’s controlled trial: experienced developers expected to be ~20% faster. They measured 19% slower. The sample was small and the result is debated, but the lesson is that perception and reality diverge.
→ Veracode: 45% of AI-generated code samples contained a security flaw.
We made one step of the SDLC dramatically faster, writing code. The constraint moved to review, testing, security and requirements.
If your PR count doubled and your lead time didn’t move, you didn’t adopt AI. You bought a faster typewriter.

Here's What Helps:  (the playbook):
Baseline the whole flow first. Track all five DORA metrics (lead time, deploy frequency, recovery time, change failure rate, rework rate) before and after rollout. Don’t track lines or PR counts.
Treat code review as the new critical path. Smaller PRs, AI-assisted first-pass review, and human attention reserved for design and risk.
Raise the gates as the volume rises. Tests, SAST and policy-as-code in CI. AI-written code gets the same bar or a stricter one.
Go beyond the IDE. Requirements, test generation, migration, docs and incident triage. Gartner projects 25–30% gains for teams applying AI across the full lifecycle, against roughly 10% for code-generation-only approaches.
Govern agents by autonomy tier. Decide what AI can suggest, what it can change, and what it can ship, with a named human owner for each.
Fix the system underneath. DORA found gains concentrate in loosely coupled architectures with fast feedback loops. AI won’t rescue a slow pipeline.
AI doesn’t fix your delivery system. It stress-tests it.
Where is your bottleneck now: review, testing, or requirements? 👇
